FunkcijeFeatures

Kako Popravko deluje.How Popravko works.

01StrankaCustomer

Slikaj, kaj je treba popravitiSnap what needs fixing

Stranka odpre Popravko, tapne »Rabim mojstra« in posname težavo: pušča cev, napihnjen laminat, pokvarjena vtičnica. Doda eno ali več slik in nekaj besed. Namesto tipkanja lahko opis tudi pove v mikrofon.

The customer opens Popravko, taps “I need a tradesperson” and takes a photo of the problem: a leaking pipe, swollen laminate, a broken socket. They add one or more photos and a few words, or simply dictate the description.

  • Brez registracije, gesla in dolgih obrazcev.No sign-up, no password, no long forms.
  • Slika ali opis, dovolj je eno od obojega.A photo or a description, either one is enough.
  • Velike črke in jasni gumbi, primerno tudi za starejše.Large type and clear buttons, suitable for older people too.
02StrankaCustomer

Popravko sam ve, kateri mojster je potrebenPopravko knows which trade you need

Iz opisa aplikacija sama prepozna stroko. »Laminat se je napihnil« pomeni talne obloge, »v kopalnici kaplja« vodovodarja. Prepozna več kot 800 besed in izrazov, tudi narečne. Kraj izpolni sama iz lokacije, stranka pa izbere, kdaj ga potrebuje in okvirno koliko je pripravljena plačati. Na koncu vpiše telefon in tapne »Pošlji mojstrom«.

The app works out the trade from the description: “the laminate has swollen” means flooring, “dripping in the bathroom” means a plumber. It understands more than 800 words and phrases, dialect included. The town is filled in from the location, the customer picks when the job is needed and a rough budget, adds a phone number and taps “Send to tradespeople”.

  • Kdaj: čim prej, ta teden, naslednji teden ali kadarkoli.When: as soon as possible, this week, next week or any time.
  • Okvirni znesek od »do 50 €« do »nad 1.500 €« ali »ne vem«.Rough budget from “up to €50” to “over €1,500” or “not sure”.
  • Lokacija se uporabi le enkrat, za ime kraja. Točne lokacije ne hranimo.Location is used once, only for the town name. The exact position is never stored.
03StrankaCustomer

Mojstri se javijo s terminomTradespeople reply with a time

Mojstri izbrane stroke v regiji stranke takoj dobijo obvestilo. Kdor ima čas, se javi s terminom in okvirno ceno. Stranka vidi, kdo se je javil: ime, podjetje, davčno številko in oznako »Podjetje preverjeno«. Izbere enega, ostali dobijo obvestilo, da je delo oddano.

Tradespeople of that trade in the customer’s region are notified immediately. Whoever has time replies with a date and a rough price. The customer sees who replied: name, company, tax number and a “Verified company” badge. They accept one and the others are told the job is taken.

  • Brez licitiranja in dražb, stranka preprosto izbere.No bidding or auctions, the customer simply chooses.
  • Za stranke je Popravko popolnoma brezplačen.Popravko is completely free for customers.
04StrankaCustomer

Veš, kdo pride in kdajYou know who is coming and when

Po izbiri stranka vidi termin, ime in telefon mojstra. Mojster jo pokliče pred prihodom. Stanje dela je vedno jasno: poslano, mojster prihaja, opravljeno.

Once accepted, the customer sees the time, the tradesperson’s name and phone number. The tradesperson calls before arriving. The job status is always clear: sent, on the way, done.

  • Gumb »Pokliči« za takojšen klic.A “Call” button to phone right away.
  • Delo lahko stranka kadarkoli zapre ali izbriše.The customer can close or delete the job at any time.
05MojsterTradesperson

Za mojstre: nova dela v bližiniFor tradespeople: new jobs nearby

Mojster tapne »Sem mojster«, izbere svoje stroke in regije ter dobi obvestilo, takoj ko nekdo v bližini potrebuje njegovo delo. Pod »Prosta dela« vidi sliko, opis, kraj, rok in okvirni znesek ter se javi z enim dotikom.

A tradesperson taps “I’m a tradesperson”, picks their trades and regions and gets notified as soon as someone nearby needs their work. Under “Open jobs” they see the photo, description, town, timing and budget, and reply with one tap.

  • Termin izbere iz prostih ur v naslednjih 7 dneh.They pick a slot from free hours in the next 7 days.
  • Ko ga stranka izbere, dobi njen telefon in gumb »Pokliči«.When chosen, they get the customer’s phone and a “Call” button.
  • Termin doda v koledar in opomnike z enim dotikom.They add the appointment to Calendar and Reminders with one tap.
  • Brez provizij in brez plačila za posamezno stranko.No commission and no pay-per-lead.
06MojsterTradesperson

Samo preverjena podjetjaVerified companies only

Vsak mojster je registriran samostojni podjetnik ali podjetje. Ob vpisu doda sliko, ime, ime podjetja in davčno številko, ki jo aplikacija preveri. Podjetje potrdi z nakazilom 1 centa z računa podjetja, zato stranka ve, kdo prihaja na njen dom.

Every tradesperson is a registered sole trader or company. When signing up they add a photo, name, company name and tax number, which the app validates. They confirm the company with a 1-cent transfer from the company account, so customers know exactly who is coming to their home.

  • Potrditev prek banke poteka samodejno.Bank confirmation happens automatically.
  • Vsako objavo in vsakega mojstra je mogoče prijaviti. Prijave pregledamo v 24 urah.Every job post and every tradesperson can be reported. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
